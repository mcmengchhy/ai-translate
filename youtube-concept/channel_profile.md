## 📋 Recommended Content Strategy (YouTube + Shorts)

1. **Shorts / Reels Strategy (Top Funnel Growth):**
   - 30 to 60-second viral drama clips / movie funny moments.
   - Fast-hook title: *“នៅពេលដែលតួឯកលាក់ក្បាច់គុន...”* or *“ឈុតឆាកកក្រើក...”*
   - Add engaging dynamic Khmer subtitles at the bottom center.

2. **Long-Form Strategy (Watch Time & Monetization):**
   - 8-15 minute movie recaps or drama story arcs.
   - Professional pacing: Intro hook (0:00-0:15) -> Story Setup -> Climax -> Outro.
